Yakitori

Classic Japanese Grilled Chicken

Yakitori is a traditional Japanese dish featuring skewered chicken grilled to perfection. Each skewer is brushed with a savory-sweet tare sauce, giving it a rich, caramelized flavor. Perfect as a snack, appetizer, or part of a main course, yakitori is simple to prepare at home and brings authentic Japanese taste straight to your table.

Popular Recipes

Yakitori (grilled chicken) Tsukune (minced meat patties) Okonomiyaki (savory pancakes) Mizutaki (chicken stew)
