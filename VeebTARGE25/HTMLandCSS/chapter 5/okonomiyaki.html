Okonomiyaki

Japanese Savory Pancakes

Okonomiyaki is a savory Japanese pancake made with cabbage, batter, and your choice of protein or vegetables. Topped with okonomiyaki sauce, mayonnaise, and bonito flakes, it’s a flavorful and satisfying dish. Fun to make at home, this pancake allows you to customize ingredients to suit your taste while enjoying an authentic Japanese experience.

Popular Recipes

Yakitori (grilled chicken) Tsukune (minced meat patties) Okonomiyaki (savory pancakes) Mizutaki (chicken stew)
